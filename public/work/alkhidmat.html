Alkhidmat Foundation — Flood Relief

Documentaries built to move donors — staying with families through the 2022 floods, from displacement to the return home.

Client
Alkhidmat Foundation
Category
Documentary
Year
2023
Role
Director, DP, Editor

01 · The brief

Alkhidmat Foundation needed films for its flood-relief work — not reports, not a montage of statistics. Something that could sit with one family long enough that a viewer would feel the scale of what was lost, and act.

“Don't make a film about a disaster. Make a film about a family that lived through one.”

02 · The approach

I spent days in flood-affected areas of southern Punjab, filming families in temporary shelters and the volunteers rebuilding around them. No voiceover. No statistics on screen. Just the people, the silence between them, and a soundtrack that doesn't tell viewers how to feel.

03 · The outcome

The films were used across Alkhidmat's fundraising channels. The working relationship grew into more than fifty short documentaries — a body of work that became the foundation of my documentary practice.
