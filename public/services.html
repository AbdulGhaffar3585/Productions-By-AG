Services

What I do.

Four disciplines, one point of contact on every project.

01 / 04 Brand Films & Founder Stories Flagship brand films, founder-led storytelling
  • 3–7 min
  • 1–3 shoot days
  • 3–5 week delivery

Cinematic films built around the story behind a brand, a founder, or a company milestone — for websites, launches, anniversaries, investor decks, and social cut-downs.

How it runs

  1. Research, interviews and creative direction
  2. 1–3 shoot days, original cinematography and lighting
  3. Edit, colour and sound design
  4. Master film plus social cut-downs
See: Total Parco — “Fueling Progress”
02 / 04 Documentary & Cause Storytelling Field documentaries for NGOs and causes
  • Field shoots
  • 2–5 days
  • 4–6 week delivery

Documentary work for NGOs, foundations, and CSR programmes — for fundraising, donor reporting, and public awareness. Built around real people, not staged scenarios.

How it runs

  1. Subject research and pre-shoot interviews
  2. Field shooting, 2–5 days depending on scope
  3. Long-form edit plus shorter campaign cuts
  4. Optional stills from the same shoot
See: Alkhidmat — Flood Relief
03 / 04 Social Content Production Ongoing content for brands
  • Monthly retainer
  • 3-month minimum
  • 8–15 deliverables / month

Reels, photography, and short-form video for Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok — consistent, high-quality output without hiring an in-house team.

How it runs

  1. Monthly planning call
  2. One production day per month
  3. 8–15 finished pieces — reels, photos, short-form video
  4. Two rounds of revisions per deliverable
See: Care Pharmacy
04 / 04 Event & Editorial Photography Event coverage and editorial stills
  • Same-day social edits
  • Full gallery in 7 working days

Coverage for conferences, launches, summits, and brand activations — fast turnaround, editorial-grade output.

How it runs

  1. Shot list agreed before the day
  2. Single or multi-day coverage
  3. Same-day social-ready selects
  4. Full edited gallery within 7 working days
See: TEDx GCU
