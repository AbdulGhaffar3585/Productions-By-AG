Brand films

A flagship film or founder story. We shape the concept, plan the shoot and edit a finished film. A typical brief runs three to five weeks with one to three shoot days.

Social campaigns

A monthly slate of short films, vertical edits and stills built around what your brand needs to say. A typical retainer covers eight to fifteen deliverables per month.

Product shoots

Still photographs and short motion studies for a launch, a product page or a social campaign. Send the product, its defining detail and where the images will run.

Documentaries

Character-led films for organisations and causes. Field production typically takes two to five days, with four to six weeks from brief to delivery.
